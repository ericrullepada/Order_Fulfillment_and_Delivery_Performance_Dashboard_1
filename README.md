# Order Fulfillment and Delivery Performance Dashboard

## **Executive Summary**

This project analyzes order fulfillment performance using Excel to identify delivery friction and operational inefficiencies. The analysis shows that most orders fall under non-delivered statuses, with issues distributed across multiple delivery stages rather than driven by a single failure point. Assembly service is associated with higher cancellation and rescheduling rates, indicating added logistical complexity, while a small group of brands and payment methods account for the majority of order volume. These insights highlight opportunities to improve fulfillment coordination and delivery efficiency.

## **Tools & Technologies Used**

•	Microsoft Excel

•	Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts

•	Calculated Fields & Helper Columns

•	Percentage-based KPIs

•	Slicers for interactive filtering


## **Business Problem Statement**

•	The business needs to understand why a high volume of orders are not successfully delivered.

Key questions include:

•	Which delivery statuses contribute most to fulfillment friction?

•	Does assembly service affect delivery outcomes?

•	Which brands and payment methods drive the highest order volume?

•	The goal is to identify operational inefficiencies that impact customer experience and order fulfillment.

## **Methodology**

•	Classified orders into Delivered and Fulfillment Friction categories.

•	Aggregated order data by delivery status, assembly service, brand, and payment method.

•	Converted raw counts into percentage-based KPIs for fair comparison.

•	Used comparative analysis to evaluate the relationship between:

•	Assembly service and delivery outcomes

•	Delivery statuses and order volume

•	Interpreted findings using business and operational context rather than time-based trends.


## **Results and Findings**

•	Only 17.8% of orders were successfully delivered, while 82.2% fell under fulfillment friction.

•	No single delivery failure dominates, indicating systemic fulfillment challenges across multiple delivery stages.

•	Orders with assembly service show higher cancellation and rescheduling rates, suggesting increased logistical complexity.

•	Orders without assembly service exhibit a higher proportion of failed deliveries.

•	Credit cards and digital wallets account for the majority of order volume.

•	A small group of brands contributes disproportionately to total orders.



## **Recomendations :**
•	Review and improve logistics processes across all delivery stages rather than focusing on a single failure point.

•	Optimize assembly service scheduling and coordination to reduce cancellations and rescheduled deliveries.

•	Investigate root causes of failed deliveries in non-assembly orders.

•	Use payment and brand performance insights to prioritize operational improvements for high-volume segments.
